# Soundboard audio

Padcade dedicates the audio from its original factory synthesizer to the public domain under
[CC0 1.0 Universal](../../../../LICENSE-sounds). This covers the drum sounds, including Classic kick and the tuned Electric kicks, the bass and other synth notes, the one-finger chords,
the nine effects, and the synthesized sounds in Memes (Record scratch, Drum roll, Laser and Ta-da). You may use the sounds in
commercial releases and share the samples. The synthesizer source code is licensed under GPL-3.0-or-later.

## Original Jam kit

The original Jam kit contains ten plucked notes, ten bass notes and eight drum hits. Each was synthesized from oscillators and filtered noise for this project. The mono WAVs preserve the kit’s original mix, with one gain adjustment to match Classic kick. The chromatic Jam notes in Synth use the same pluck and bass recipes and gain. All of these sounds are CC0.

The early Padcade Bass and Pluck notes use a plucked-string model with a seeded burst of noise and a short fade at each end. Those notes and the original Hat and Tom are also CC0.

## Piano recordings in Keys

Keys uses 16 recordings from the softer velocity layer of FreePats **Upright Piano KW** (2022-02-21).
Gonzalo and Roberto recorded the acoustic upright in January 2017. Roberto edited the samples. The authors
dedicated this sound bank to the public domain under CC0 1.0.
[Original recordings and dedication](https://github.com/freepats/upright-piano-KW/tree/570f6c60ed2eff67accad3b85d5b452e57a3ad28).

The bundled source notes come from C, D♯, F♯ and A in octaves 2 through 5 (`vL.flac` files in `samples/`).
Padcade fine-tunes the notes to concert pitch, mixes them to mono at 22,050 Hz, applies a common gain
and fades the ends of the recordings. Each keeps up to six seconds of the original performance.
The board builds the 48 playable stereo keys offline from these recordings. The adaptations are also CC0.

the site's previews, cut to length, evened out in loudness and saved with one channel. Thank you to their authors.
