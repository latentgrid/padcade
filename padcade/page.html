Password for this page

iWithout a password, anyone on your network can play and manage the board. A password locks the whole page. Open the page for playing afterwards, and the password is only asked for managing the board: plugins, connections, updates and resets.

The board as a device in Home Assistant (MQTT)

iWith a broker the board shows up in Home Assistant as one device: pads on or off, the page, brightness, volume and battery. Leave the address empty to do without. It starts with the board, so a change here counts from the board's next start. This is separate from the Home Assistant page, which puts your lights on the pads.

Owner sign-in

On the Pi, run cd /opt/padcade and python3 -m padcade --set-admin-password as the installing user. Then sign in with that password. Players on the LAN can keep using the pads.
